This sheet records choices for an authorized release owner. Recommendations are not approvals. S22R17 records Model B — Public Technical Preview as the selected target only. No other authorization is inferred.

| Decision | Evidence and recommendation | Alternatives, consequence, and engineering dependency | Approval status and source |

## Required owner responses

For each pending row, record approve, reject, or request remediation; identify the approving owner and date. For distribution authenticity, also name the signing-key custodian, trusted distribution location, and independent route for publishing the key fingerprint. For publication, record the exact ISO SHA-256, version, authorized channel, and person executing the release.

The recommendations above are not waivers. If the owner rejects a proposed exclusion, its engineering dependency becomes a release requirement.
